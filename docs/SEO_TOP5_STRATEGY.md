# SEO Top-5 Strategy — Promtagram

## Status

`SEO_TOP5_STRATEGY_READY / CASE_ENGINE_REQUIRED`

## Date

2026-05-12

## Goal

Bring Promtagram into visible search positions first for commercial long-tail and middle-tail queries, then build authority for broad head terms like:

- господдержка;
- поддержка бизнеса;
- господдержка бизнеса;
- субсидии для бизнеса.

## SERP diagnosis

### Head terms

Queries like `господдержка`, `поддержка бизнеса`, `господдержка бизнеса` are dominated by:

- official state platforms;
- regional My Business portals;
- banks;
- legal/information portals;
- large media and business publications.

Promtagram should not fight these queries directly at the current stage.

### Winnable zone

The winnable zone is commercial-intent and problem-intent search:

- ФРП для производства;
- документы для ФРП;
- бизнес-план для ФРП;
- субсидии на оборудование;
- господдержка автоматизации производства;
- поддержка АПК переработка;
- льготный лизинг оборудования;
- РЭЦ экспортная поддержка;
- инвестплан для господдержки;
- как проверить меры поддержки для бизнеса.

## Strategic thesis

Promtagram should become not a generic `господдержка` page, but a practical AI-assisted navigation hub:

`measure → project type → documents → risks → preliminary scoring → next action`

## Pareto actions

### 1. Case engine

Create `/cases/` and publish first 5 case-based materials:

1. ФРП для производства: что проверить до подачи заявки.
2. Субсидии на оборудование: документы, сроки и риски.
3. АПК и переработка: сырьевая база, оборудование, регион.
4. Автоматизация производства: роботизация, эффект, меры поддержки.
5. Лизинг оборудования: техзадание, поставщик, компенсации.

Each case must be labeled:

- Собственный кейс;
- Публичный источник;
- Реконструкция;
- Демо;
- Гипотеза;
- Требует проверки.

### 2. Cluster expansion

Expand existing pages from thin MVP pages to strong SEO pages:

- `/frp/` first;
- `/subsidies/` second;
- `/automation/` third;
- `/apk/` fourth;
- `/leasing/` fifth.

Each page should contain:

- 800–1500 words;
- table of checks;
- document checklist;
- risk block;
- FAQ;
- internal links;
- CTA to preliminary scoring.

### 3. Distribution loop

Every article/case becomes:

- site page;
- Telegram post;
- VK post;
- Dzen article;
- VC.ru or TenChat post;
- short video script;
- PDF checklist.

### 4. Search Console iteration

Weekly:

- check queries;
- check pages with impressions but low CTR;
- update titles/H1/FAQ;
- add internal links;
- improve pages that Google discovered but did not index.

### 5. Trust and evidence

Create `/evidence/` and explain:

- how Promtagram marks cases;
- why reconstructions are not claimed as own cases;
- how official sources are used;
- how risk QA protects clients.

## What not to do

Do not use:

- click manipulation;
- mass AI pages without value;
- doorway pages;
- copied official texts;
- fake cases;
- guaranteed approval claims;
- paid link spam;
- third-party parasite SEO.

## Top-5 requirement

To reach Top 5, Promtagram needs:

1. Topical depth around government support routes.
2. Case-based content unavailable on generic portals.
3. Clear trust/evidence discipline.
4. Regular indexed content.
5. Internal linking between clusters and cases.
6. External references from social platforms, partner pages, and expert publications.
7. No manipulative SEO.
8. CRM feedback loop to identify pages that bring qualified leads.
