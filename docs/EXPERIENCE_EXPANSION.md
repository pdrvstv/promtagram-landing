# Promtagram: achievements, anonymized cases and ecosystem

## Intent / authorization
User requested public achievements, anonymized cases from own practice, iTunes-like carousel, separate pages, topical imagery, organization strip, smooth transitions and navigation updates. Informational publication authorized. No new intake/data processing. Historical intake HOLD remains unchanged.

## Content and evidence decisions
- Five generalized case studies from the archived working CRM report of 05.09.2026. Amounts are needs, not amounts received. Names, contacts, geography, entity identifiers, private financial figures and internal IDs excluded. Each card distinguishes completed preparation from proposed checks and next step. Historical snapshot date is visible on detail/archive pages; no assertion of present live deal status.
- Achievements use published Leader-ID programmes 565362 and 565889, expert-club profile 5044 and existing media. Gosneyroset presented as a concept. Unverified reach, awards and funding outcomes excluded.
- Institutions labelled as ecosystem, not partners. No evidence supports claiming cooperation with every government body. Verified professional roles are separately described on achievements page.
- Nine key ecosystem entries plus explanation of regional funds/guarantee/microfinance/export bodies. Three source SVG logos (MSP, My Business, ASI); other names displayed as text rather than fabricated logos.
